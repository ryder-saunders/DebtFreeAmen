# Section Templates

Use these as the baseline landing-page blocks before creating new variants.
The matching typed reference lives in `src/lib/section-templates.ts`.

## Hero With Lead Form

- Non-sticky header with the white logo and phone CTA.
- Navy image-overlay hero.
- Left side: headline, subcopy, qualify CTA, phone CTA, trust chips.
- Right side: private survey or lead form.
- Component reference: `src/components/hero.tsx`.

## Value Cards

- Short heading and body.
- Three compact cards.
- Standalone check icons, not icon boxes.
- Component reference: `src/components/value-section.tsx`.

## Process Steps

- White or light tan band.
- Three numbered steps.
- CTA near the section heading.
- Component reference: `src/components/process-section.tsx`.

## Image Proof Split

- Realistic River Relief image on one side.
- Heading, short body, and proof bullets on the other.
- Keep mobile image crops compact.
- Component reference: `src/components/proof-section.tsx`.

## Final CTA Band

- Brand-blue full-width band.
- Short promise-oriented heading.
- Primary qualify CTA and secondary phone CTA.
- Component reference: `src/components/final-cta.tsx`.
